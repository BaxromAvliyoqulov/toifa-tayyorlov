# 📚 2-KUN: Conditionals (Shart Gaplar & Mixed Conditionals) & TBL Metodika

## 📌 Mavzu: Shart ergash gaplarning 4 turi, aralash shart gaplar tuzoqlari va TBL dars modeli
* **Toifa talabi:** Mutaxassis ➔ 2-Toifa
* **Kunlik ajratilgan vaqt:** 2 soat (120 daqiqa)

---

## 🧐 Darsning Asosiy Nazariy Qismlari:
### 1. 0, 1, 2, 3 Conditionals
* **Zero Conditional (Umumiy haqiqat va ilmiy faktlar):** `If + Present Simple, ... Present Simple`
  * *Misol:* If you heat ice, it melts.
* **First Conditional (Kelajakdagi real ehtimol):** `If + Present Simple, ... will / can / may + V1`
  * *Misol:* If she prepares thoroughly, she will pass the toifa exam.
  * *Tuzoq:* If qismida hech qachon `will` ishlatilmaydi! (Xato: *If she will prepare ❌*)
* **Second Conditional (Hozirgi/kelasi zamondagi noreal xayol):** `If + Past Simple, ... would / could / might + V1`
  * *Misol:* If I had enough time, I would write a book. (Rasmiy grammatikada barcha shaxslar uchun `were` afzal: *If I were you...*)
* **Third Conditional (O'tmishdagi noreal afsus va pushaymonlik):** `If + Past Perfect (had + V3), ... would have + V3`
  * *Misol:* If they had left earlier, they wouldn't have missed the flight.

### 2. Mixed Conditionals (Aralash Shart Gaplar)
* **1-Tur (O'tmishdagi harakat ➔ Hozirgi natija):**
  * `If + Past Perfect (had + V3), ... would + V1 (now)`
  * *Misol:* If I had taken my umbrella this morning, I wouldn't be wet now.
* **2-Tur (Hozirgi doimiy xususiyat ➔ O'tmishdagi natija):**
  * `If + Past Simple (were / V2), ... would have + V3 (yesterday)`
  * *Misol:* If he were not so stubborn, he would have accepted their help yesterday.

### 3. Inversion in Conditionals (If-omission)
* **Type 1:** `Should you require further information...` (= If you require...)
* **Type 2:** `Were I in your shoes...` / `Were he to ask...` (= If I were... / If he asked...)
* **Type 3:** `Had he known the exam date...` (= If he had known...)
* *Inkor qolipi:* `Had it not been for your advice...` (Xato: *Hadn't it been for... ❌*)

### 4. Bog'lovchilar va Tuzoqlar
* **Unless (= If not):** O'zida inkor bor, undan keyin inkor fe'l kelmaydi! (*Unless you study hard, you won't pass.*)
* **In case vs If:** *In case* = Har ehtimolga qarshi oldindan chora ko'rish; *If* = Faqat shart yuz berganda.

### 5. TBL (Task-Based Learning) Modeli
* **1. Pre-Task:** Mavzuni ochish, kontekst yaratish, vazifa maqsadini tushuntirish.
* **2. Task Cycle:** 
  * *Task:* Juftlik/guruhda vazifani erkin bajarish (fluency ustuvor).
  * *Planning:* Natijani sinfga taqdim etishni rejalashtirish.
  * *Report:* Sinf oldida taqdimot qilish.
* **3. Language Focus:**
  * *Analysis:* Vazifa davomida yuzaga kelgan grammatik/leksik xatolarni tahlil qilish.
  * *Practice:* Qoidalarni mustahkamlovchi mashqlar.
* **Farqi:** PPP deduktiv (qoida oldin o'tiladi), TBL induktiv (til tahlili oxirida Language Focus'da qilinadi).

## ⚡ Eng Katta Attestatsiya Tuzog'i:
> **DIQQAT:** 'Unless'dan keyin inkor fe'l (don't) qo'yilmaydi! Fe'l ijobiy bo'ladi. Third conditional inversiyasida 'Had it not been for...' ishlatiladi (hadn't qisqartmasi xato).
