# 📚 17-KUN: Adjectives vs Adverbs & Degree Modifiers & Differentsiatsiya

* **Toifa talabi:** Mutaxassis ➔ 2-Toifa
* **Kunlik ajratilgan vaqt:** 2 soat (120 daqiqa)

## 🧐 Darsning Asosiy Nazariy Qismlari:
### 1. Adjectives after sense verbs (smell delicious, feel good)
O'zbek tiliga qiyoslagan holda sodda va ravon tushuntirilgan qoidalar, hayotiy misollar va formulalar.

### 2. Flat adverbs (fast, hard, late)
